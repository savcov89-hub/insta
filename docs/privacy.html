Privacy Policy — Reels Savtsov

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Reels Savtsov is a private tool used only by Mikhail Savtsov to manage his own Instagram professional account (@fitness_savtsov). It is not offered to other users.

What data the app accesses

  • Media published on @fitness_savtsov and their statistics (views, reach, likes, comments count, saves, shares).
  • Comments left under these media: comment text, username of the commenter, time and like count. They are used only to read and answer questions from viewers.
  • Audience statistics of the account (city, age, gender in aggregate).

How the data is used

To publish Reels on schedule, to see how each Reel performs, and to read and answer comments under the account's own posts. Data is not sold, not shared with third parties and not used for advertising.

Storage

Aggregated statistics are stored in the account owner's repository. Comments are collected once a day and kept in the account owner's repository for up to 90 days after they were written (longer only while still waiting for an answer), then deleted automatically.

Data deletion

Anyone who wants their comment data removed can follow the instructions on the data deletion page.

Contact

Mikhail Savtsov, Instagram: @fitness_savtsov.

Кратко по-русски

Приложение Reels Savtsov нужно только Михаилу Савцову для своего аккаунта @fitness_savtsov: выкладка роликов, статистика и чтение комментариев под своими публикациями, чтобы отвечать на вопросы. Данные никому не передаются и не продаются. Удалить свои данные можно по инструкции на странице удаления данных.
